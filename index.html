The problem

Buying a used motorcycle in Egypt runs on trust you can't verify.

A buyer who wants one honest listing often has to pay for several inspections first, because some sellers describe the bike's condition however suits the sale.

The old way

  • ✕Seller's word is the only information you get before visiting.
  • ✕You pay a technician out of pocket, every single visit.
  • ✕If the bike turns out misrepresented, that inspection fee is gone.
  • ✕Honest sellers have no way to prove they're honest.

With Bikora

  • ✓Every listing includes a full seller-declared inspection, item by item.
  • ✓Your technician's findings are compared automatically against it.
  • ✓If the seller misrepresented the bike, the inspection fee comes out of money the seller already put on hold.
  • ✓If the seller was honest, nothing changes — you simply pay for the inspection as agreed.
How it works

One booking, one inspection, one automatic comparison.

Every step happens inside the app. No cash changes hands for the booking itself — only the pre-agreed inspection fee, and only when it's actually owed.

01
Seller

Lists the bike with a full inspection

The seller fills in a complete, itemized checklist — engine, chassis, brakes, electrics and more — declaring the honest condition of each part before the listing goes live.

02
Buyer

Books an inspection day

The buyer picks one of the seller's available days and proposes an inspection fee. The seller accepts or rejects the request.

03
Both

A technician inspects, on the spot

On the agreed day, the buyer's own technician examines the bike and fills in a fresh inspection — right there, next to the seller's original declaration.

04
System

Compares and settles automatically

The two inspections are compared item by item. The outcome — who pays what — follows a fixed rule, not a negotiation.

What happens when they disagree

The cost of a bad inspection moves to whoever was dishonest.

Before accepting a booking, the seller places the inspection fee on hold. If the technician's findings don't match the seller's declaration, that held amount settles the difference — automatically, without either side needing to argue about it.
